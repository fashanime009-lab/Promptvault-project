The Midjourney Prompt Formula for Consistent, Professional Results

Why your Midjourney results look random from prompt to prompt, and the five-part structure professional AI artists use to get consistent, gallery-quality output.

If your Midjourney results feel like a slot machine — great one prompt, muddy the next — the problem is usually missing structure, not bad luck.

The five layers of a strong prompt

  • Subject — what's actually in frame, described concretely.
  • Style — photography, painting, 3D render, illustration, and by whom or in what era.
  • Lighting — golden hour, studio softbox, harsh noon sun, neon — lighting does more work than any other single word.
  • Composition — camera angle, lens, framing (close-up, wide, aerial).
  • Parameters — aspect ratio (--ar), stylization, and version flags at the end.

Why order matters

Midjourney weighs earlier words in a prompt more heavily. Put your subject first, parameters last, and keep style, lighting, and composition in the middle — reordering the same words can noticeably change the output.

A worked example

Weak: "a woman, portrait, nice lighting". Strong: "editorial portrait of a woman, soft window light, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, muted color palette --ar 4:5". Same idea, dramatically more consistent results.

Building a repeatable style

Once you find a combination of style, lighting, and composition words that works, reuse that exact phrasing across a project — consistency comes from repeating a formula, not from luck.
