8 Free Ways to Use AI for Small Business Marketing in 2026

Practical, no-budget ways small business owners are using ChatGPT to do the marketing work they'd otherwise pay an agency for.

Hiring a marketing agency starts at hundreds of dollars a month. For most small businesses, ChatGPT and a few free tools can cover a surprising share of that work — if you know what to ask for.

Eight things you can hand to AI this week

  • A week of social captions — generate 7 at once, then edit for voice instead of starting from a blank page.
  • A first-draft email campaign — welcome series, re-engagement, or a holiday promo, ready to personalize.
  • Blog post titles and outlines — the slowest part of content marketing is deciding what to write, not writing it.
  • A customer FAQ page — feed it your common support questions, get back clear, on-brand answers.
  • Ad copy variations — generate 5 versions of one ad to test instead of guessing which angle works.
  • A competitor comparison outline — structure, not opinion; you fill in the specifics.
  • A press release draft for a launch or milestone — the format is formulaic, which is exactly what AI is good at.
  • A referral or loyalty program description — turn a rough idea into copy customers will actually read.

The one rule that matters

AI drafts, you decide. The fastest way to sound generic is publishing the first output verbatim — the value is in how much editing time it saves you, not in skipping the edit entirely.

Where to start

If you're not sure what to ask for, start from prompts that already work and swap in your own business details — that's faster than writing from a blank page, and it's exactly what the free marketing prompt pack linked below is for.
